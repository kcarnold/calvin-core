Unofficial

Workday is the official record. Your Academic Progress page in Workday is the system of record for your core requirements. This page is a planning aid: if it disagrees with Workday, go with Workday and ask your advisor or the Registrar.

Requirements here are copied from the Calvin catalog as saved on . Use the catalog for the year you entered Calvin. Each sentence of catalog wording is marked as checked or not checked by this tool.

Paste your courses from Workday

  1. In Workday, open Academic Record, then Academic Progress (or Academic History).
  2. If a table has several pages, set it to show all rows.
  3. Select all (Ctrl/⌘-A), copy, and paste below.

Everything runs in your browser. What you paste isn’t uploaded or saved anywhere, and is gone when you close or reload this page.
